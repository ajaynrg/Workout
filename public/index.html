Safety Checklist & Longevity
Rules for injury-free lifetime training

When to Skip / Rest

  • Sharp joint pain: Stop immediately and rest 2–3 days.
  • Fever or illness: Full recovery rest.
  • Under 5 hours sleep: Switch to light mobility or walking.
