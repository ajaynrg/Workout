# 5-DAY BALANCED STRENGTH PROGRAM

Progressive Overload • Same Exercises • Lifetime Training[cite: 1]

---

## PROGRAM OVERVIEW

* **Training Days:** Monday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday[cite: 1]
* **Rest Days:** Tuesday, Wednesday[cite: 1]
* **Goal:** Balanced physique development | Injury-free training | Lifetime sustainability[cite: 1]

---

## PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD STRATEGY

* **Week 1-4:** Establish baseline with working weights[cite: 1].
* **Week 5+:** Add 0.5–1 kg to dumbbells every 1–2 weeks OR add 1–2 reps before increasing weight[cite: 1].
* **Every 4 weeks:** Deload week (same weight, 20% fewer reps) for recovery[cite: 1].
* **Tracking:** Write down weight, reps, and sets after each exercise[cite: 1]. Progress = more weight or more reps = success[cite: 1].

## MONDAY: PUSH DAY
**Focus:** Chest, Shoulders, Triceps | **Duration:** 50–60 minutes[cite: 1]

### Monday Warm-Up (5–8 Minutes)
* **Arm Circles & Cross-Body Swings:** 15 forward, 15 backward, 15 across chest.
* **Scapular Push-Ups:** 2 sets × 10–12 reps (hands on floor/bench; isolate shoulder blade movement).
* **Band Pull-Aparts or Light Dumbbell Face Pulls:** 2 sets × 15 reps.
* **Warm-Up Sets:** 1–2 light acclimatization sets of Incline Dumbbell Press (50% working weight × 8 reps; 75% working weight × 4 reps) before set 1.

---

### 1. DUMBBELL INCLINE BENCH PRESS
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 4 × 6–8 (Heavy)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 2–3 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5–1 kg when you hit 8 reps on all sets[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Bench inclined 30–45 degrees (feet flat on floor)[cite: 1]
* Chest up, shoulders packed back (don't shrug toward ears)[cite: 1]
* Lower dumbbells in a controlled arc — elbows 45° from body[cite: 1]
* Press straight up, NOT toward the ceiling — exhale as you press[cite: 1]
* Full stretch at bottom (feel chest stretch), no bouncing[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS (Stop if you feel these):**
* 🚩 Sharp pain in shoulders (not normal soreness) → STOP, rest 2–3 days[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Wrist bending backward (dumbbells tilting) → Grip tighter, align wrist straight[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Elbows flaring past 90° (going too wide) → Move elbows closer to ribs[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Arching lower back excessively → Reduce weight, engage core[cite: 1]

---

### 2. DUMBBELL FLAT BENCH PRESS
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 8–10 (Moderate)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 2 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5 kg or 1–2 reps every 2 weeks[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Flat bench — same shoulder packing as incline[cite: 1]
* Dumbbells should touch lightly at the top (don't lock elbows)[cite: 1]
* Lower to chest level — controlled, ~2 seconds down[cite: 1]
* Press explosively but controlled (no jerking)[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Dumbbells drifting inward at top (pressing toward center) → Press straight up[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Chest caving inward — shoulders collapsing forward → Reduce weight immediately[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Bench unstable or wobbly → Check bench is secure before pressing[cite: 1]

---

### 3. DUMBBELL SHOULDER PRESS
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 8 (Moderate-Heavy)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 2 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5 kg every 2–3 weeks[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Seated on bench, back support — dumbbells at shoulder height[cite: 1]
* Elbows slightly in front of shoulders (NOT flared out to sides)[cite: 1]
* Press straight up — dumbbells should meet above head (don't collide)[cite: 1]
* Lower with control — dumbbells return to shoulder height[cite: 1]
* Core tight — no excessive lower back arch[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Sharp shoulder pain (front or top of shoulder) → REDUCE WEIGHT or SKIP[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Neck tension or feeling pain in neck/upper traps → Lower dumbbells, don't shrug up[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Dumbbells pressing forward (not straight up) → Reposition elbows forward[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Lower back hyperextending (excessive arch) → Reduce weight, engage abs[cite: 1]

---

### 4. DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISES
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 12–15 (Light)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 1–1.5 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5 kg OR 2–3 reps[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Stand with light dumbbells at sides, slight bend in elbows (20° bend, NOT locked)[cite: 1]
* Raise dumbbells out to sides in a wide arc until arms are parallel to ground[cite: 1]
* Thumb slightly lower than pinky (like pouring a can) — NOT thumbs up[cite: 1]
* Lower with control, no swinging — exhale on the way up[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Using momentum/swinging body → Reduce weight significantly[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Shrugging shoulders up toward ears → Keep shoulders down, engage lats[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Dumbbells going higher than shoulder height → Stop at shoulder level[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Neck pain or shoulder impingement → Stop, rest 2–3 days[cite: 1]

---

### 5. DUMBBELL OVERHEAD TRICEPS EXTENSION
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 10–12 (Moderate)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 1.5 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5 kg OR 2 reps every 2 weeks[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Seated or standing (core engaged) — dumbbell held with both hands[cite: 1]
* Dumbbell behind head (one end down, like holding an upside-down bell)[cite: 1]
* Elbows tucked, close to sides — NOT flaring out[cite: 1]
* Lower dumbbell behind head with a deep stretch — press up to full arm extension[cite: 1]
* Keep elbows stationary throughout (don't let them move forward/back)[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Elbow pain (inside of elbow) → STOP immediately, may be tendonitis[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Elbows flaring outward → Keep them locked in place[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Neck strain or dumbbell hitting head → Use lighter weight, better control[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Lower back arching excessively → Reduce weight or sit upright with support[cite: 1]

## THURSDAY: PULL DAY
**Focus:** Back, Biceps | **Duration:** 50–60 minutes[cite: 1]

### Thursday Warm-Up (5–8 Minutes)
* **Dead Hangs (Pull-up Bar):** 2 sets × 20–30 seconds (spine and shoulder decompression).
* **Cat-Cow Stretches:** 10 cycles (spinal extension/flexion to prep for rows).
* **Bird-Dogs:** 2 sets × 8 reps per side (activates erectors and deep core stabilizers).
* **Warm-Up Sets:** 1–2 sets of Bent-Over Dumbbell Rows with 50% load × 8 reps before working sets.

---

### 1. DUMBBELL BENT-OVER ROWS
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 4 × 6–8 (Heavy)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 2–3 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5–1 kg when you hit 8 reps[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Hinge forward 45–90° at hips (keep chest roughly parallel to ground or slightly higher)[cite: 1]
* Knees slightly bent (10–20° bend, NOT locked, NOT deep squat)[cite: 1]
* Neutral spine — don't round your lower back (tighten abs)[cite: 1]
* Row dumbbells up to ribs — elbows tight to body, NOT flaring out[cite: 1]
* Squeeze shoulder blades together at top — 1-second hold[cite: 1]
* Lower with control — dumbbells hang straight below shoulders[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Lower back rounding or pain → Reduce hinge angle, reduce weight[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Elbows flaring out wide → Keep elbows close to ribs[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Using momentum to swing dumbbells up → Reduce weight, row slower[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Dumbbells drifting in front of body → Keep them directly below shoulders[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Shoulder blade not retracting (no squeeze at top) → Focus on pulling shoulder blades back[cite: 1]

---

### 2. DUMBBELL SINGLE-ARM ROWS
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 8–10 per arm (Moderate)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 1.5 minutes per arm[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5 kg every 2 weeks[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Half-kneeling position: one knee down, one leg forward, hips squared forward[cite: 1]
* Row with arm opposite the forward leg (left leg forward = right arm rows)[cite: 1]
* Keep torso stable — no twisting or rotating[cite: 1]
* Pull dumbbell up to ribs, elbow close to body[cite: 1]
* Squeeze lat at top — feel the contraction on the side you're rowing[cite: 1]
* Lower with control, arm returns to full extension[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Rotating or twisting torso → Keep core engaged, shoulders facing forward[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Hip hiking or tilting to one side → Maintain level hips[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Dumbbell drifting away from body → Row close to ribs[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Shoulder elevation (hiking up) → Keep shoulders down[cite: 1]

---

### 3. INVERTED ROWS (Pull-up Bar)
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 8–10 (Moderate)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 2 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Lower body angle (make it harder) every 2 weeks[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Hang under bar with hands shoulder-width apart, body straight (plank position)[cite: 1]
* Feet elevated on bench or ground depending on difficulty (higher feet = harder)[cite: 1]
* Body should form a straight line from heels to head — NO hips sagging[cite: 1]
* Pull chest up to bar — elbows close to body (NOT flaring)[cite: 1]
* Squeeze shoulder blades and lats at top — 1-second hold[cite: 1]
* Lower with control until arms are straight[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Hips sagging or lower back hyperextending → Keep body rigid, tighten core[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Using momentum or swinging → Perform slow, controlled reps[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Elbows flaring out wide → Keep them close to ribs[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Shoulder impingement or pain → Reduce difficulty (raise body angle)[cite: 1]

---

### 4. DUMBBELL BICEP CURLS
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 10–12 (Moderate)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 1.5 minutes[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5 kg OR 2 reps every 2–3 weeks[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Stand with dumbbells at sides, elbows locked at 90° (slightly in front of hips)[cite: 1]
* Curl dumbbells up smoothly by bending elbows — DON'T move shoulders[cite: 1]
* Bring dumbbells toward shoulders — feel the bicep contract[cite: 1]
* Control the lowering — ~2 seconds down (eccentric is important for growth)[cite: 1]
* Dumbbells return to sides without locking elbows (slight bend at bottom)[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Swinging dumbbells up (using momentum) → Reduce weight, control the lift[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Shoulders shrugging up → Keep shoulders down and back[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Elbows drifting away from body → Keep elbows tight to sides[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Wrist flexing backward → Keep wrists neutral, don't bend[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Elbow pain (inner elbow) → STOP, rest 2–3 days, use lighter weight[cite: 1]

---

### 5. DUMBBELL HAMMER CURLS
| Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sets × Reps** | 3 × 12–15 (Light-Moderate)[cite: 1] |
| **Rest Between Sets** | 1 minute[cite: 1] |
| **Progression** | Add 0.5 kg OR 3 reps every 2–3 weeks[cite: 1] |

**FORM CUES:**
* Dumbbells held vertically (like holding hammers/handles facing forward)[cite: 1]
* Elbows locked at 90° at sides — same position as standard curls[cite: 1]
* Curl dumbbells up — thumbs face forward throughout (neutral grip)[cite: 1]
* Feel the contraction in biceps AND outer forearms (brachioradialis)[cite: 1]
* Lower with control — same path, smooth tempo[cite: 1]

**RED FLAGS:**
* 🚩 Twisting dumbbells (rotating grip) → Keep grip neutral throughout[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Using momentum to swing up → Reduce weight, perform slower[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Elbows moving forward/backward → Keep them stationary[cite: 1]
* 🚩 Wrist pain → Ensure dumbbells are held vertically, not tilted[cite: 1]
