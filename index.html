Warm-Up Protocol (5-7 minutes)
Progression Guidelines
Week 1–2: Form Focus Use lighter weights, focus on perfect form and full ROM, build mind-muscle connection.
Week 3–4: Establish Baseline Find weights for target reps with good form, aim for pump/tightness, track all weights used.
Week 5–8: Progressive Overload Add 1–2 reps per set (increase weight by 5% when all target reps hit), maintain form quality.
Form > Load Always If form breaks down, reduce weight immediately. Sharp sensations require immediate halt.
Tips for Success
1. Warm-Up Thoroughly Prepares specific muscle groups and joint capsules prior to heavy load.
2. Rest Intervals 60-90 seconds between compound sets, 45-60 seconds between isolation movements.
3. Track Everything Record weights, reps, and RPE for objective progressive overload.
4. Adequate Protein Consume 0.8-1g protein per pound of bodyweight on training days.
5. Sleep Recovery Target 7-9 hours to maximize hormonal synthesis and tissue repair.
6. Maintain Hydration Keep fluid and electrolyte intake consistent across your session.
7. Planned Deloads Reduce overall volume by 40-50% every 8 weeks to reset CNS fatigue.
8. Listen to Feedback Differentiate productive muscular soreness from joint or sharp tendon pain.
