8-Week Progression Framework
Weeks 1–2: Form Foundation Movement quality, technique mastery, baseline establishment. Use lighter weight than you think you need. Form > load.
Weeks 3–4: Baseline Calibration Lock in optimal loads where target reps are completed with clean form. Maintain 2-second eccentric lowering tempo.
Weeks 5–8: Progressive Overload Add 1–2 reps per set or increase weight load by 5% upon reaching repetition targets.
Form > Load Always If form breaks down or lower back arches uncontrollably, reduce weight immediately. Sharp pain requires immediate halt.
Training Notes & Best Practices
1. Controlled Tempo 2-second eccentric (lowering) descent across all press and curl movements to maximize mechanical tension.
2. Rest Intervals 75–90 seconds for compound presses, squats, and rows; 60 seconds for isolation triceps, biceps, and lateral raises.
3. Fixed 6.5ft Bar Consideration Low-bar movements like inverted rows have been replaced with dumbbell rows and seated band work to accommodate your high fixed bar.
4. Adequate Nutrition Support progressive overload with sufficient protein and water intake across your training split.
